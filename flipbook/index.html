Sunroom Paint Options

Flip with the arrow keys, the buttons, or a swipe. The view along the wall looks toward the windows, where glare on paint shows most.

All four are shown at midday in fall. Compare them for color: exposure is set per option, so brightness differs from one to the next.

Walls are eggshell in option 1 and matte in the others. Trim and crown are semi-gloss. Doors are satin, except semi-gloss in option 2. Windows are white in every option, handles are matte black and the floor is the same walnut tone. Option 2 has a woven dome light; the others have a black globe.

Colors are screen values for the named chips, and option 2 uses design-target colors. Brush a real sample in the room before buying.
