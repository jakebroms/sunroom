Sunroom Options

Flip with the arrow keys, the buttons, or a swipe. Within a room, the camera, light and exposure stay the same, so what changes is the floor, or the ceiling in the last frame of each room. The view along the wall looks toward the windows.

All frames are shown at midday in fall. Each room is exposed on its own, so compare within a room rather than brightness across rooms.

Floors are the three Minwax stains on white oak, colored to the stain chart. The ceiling frames paint the ceiling the wall color at the walls' sheen; the crown stays in the trim color. Billiard Green has eggshell walls, the other two rooms matte; trim and crown are semi-gloss and doors satin, except the brown trim, which is satin. Windows are white, handles are matte black, and the light is a black globe.

Colors are screen values for the named chips. Put real samples in the room before deciding.
