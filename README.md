# Our sunroom

Renders of our sunroom remodel in fall and winter, at three times of day: greige walls, oak LVP, white doors with satin brass hardware, rattan furniture and plants.

The page is `index.html`, served by GitHub Pages. Renders made in Blender from a 3D scan of the room.

Plant, pillow, basket and prop models and the textures are CC0 from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com).
